About IPL 2026

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is India's most popular T20 cricket league. Ten franchise teams compete in a high-energy tournament played across iconic stadiums throughout the country.

Stadium Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Carry your e-ticket, either printed or on your phone. The QR code is scanned at the entry gate, and each ticket is valid for one entry only. Please arrive at least one hour before the match starts.

ID Proof Requirement

Every spectator must carry a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN Card, Driving Licence, Passport or Voter ID) that matches the name on the booking.

Prohibited Items

  • Bags, backpacks and helmets
  • Outside food, drinks and glass bottles
  • Power banks, lighters and coins
  • Laser pointers, selfie sticks and fireworks
